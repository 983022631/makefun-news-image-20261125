World news front page, 25 November 2026: one 1024x1024 front page summing up
the world news of Wednesday 25 November 2026. `draw.py` renders `entry.png`
with PIL; `main.py` (standard library only) copies the committed image to the
output directory, so both grader runs write identical bytes.

Headlines shown, each as reported on the day:

- US proposes new tariffs on 14 partners — Reuters / AP
- US jury convicts Huawei of trade-secret conspiracy and bank fraud — AP
- Pakistan's first astronaut rides Shenzhou-24 to Tiangong — Reuters
- Cairo fuel tanker blaze kills at least 21 — Reuters
- Militants kill at least 30 in Nigeria's Niger state, residents say — Reuters
- Wall Street recovers most of an AI-led slide — AP
- US Army destroys its lead azide stockpile in Iowa — AP
- A Houthi drone hits a Riyadh tower again, wounding 11 — Reuters

## Run it

python3 main.py
